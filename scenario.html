At the Supermarket

Navigating a grocery store in a new country can be overwhelming. In this scenario, you will learn how to locate specific items, ask for assistance with pricing, and successfully complete a checkout transaction at a typical academic-area supermarket.

Mentor's Note

"In most academic towns, students are expected to bring their own reusable bags or pay a small fee for paper ones. Don't be surprised if the cashier asks: 'Do you need a bag today?'"
